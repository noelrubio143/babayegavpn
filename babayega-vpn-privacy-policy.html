Last updated: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BABAYEGA VPN ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our virtual private network (VPN) application, available on the Amazon Appstore (package name: babayegavpn.ambersvpn.shop). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

Our advertising partner(s) may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and general usage data to serve and measure ads. These partners operate under their own privacy policies. 4. Location Detection

The App may use approximate location data (such as country or region, derived from your network connection, not GPS) solely to recommend the nearest available VPN server location or to display relevant server options. This information is not used to track your precise whereabouts and is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Because the App does not require an account, there is no persistent user profile tied to your identity to delete. Any locally stored app settings or cached data can be removed at any time by clearing the App's data/cache in your device settings or by uninstalling the App. If you believe any data associated with your device has been collected and would like it removed, you may contact us using the details below and we will address your request.
